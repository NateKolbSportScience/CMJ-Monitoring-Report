Put your CMJ exports (.csv) from VALD Hub or Hawkin Dynamics in this folder.

- One file or many: every CSV in this folder (and any folders inside it) is loaded.
- Each week, just save the new export here and click Refresh in your report.
- Exports that overlap (the same tests in two files) are fine; duplicates are removed.

The first time you open "CMJ Monitoring Report.pbit", it asks for DataFolder:
paste this folder's path (click the address bar at the top of this window and copy it).

This folder stays on your computer. Nothing in it is uploaded anywhere.
